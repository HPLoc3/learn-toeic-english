HUONG DAN THEM UNIT MOI (vi du: l02)

1. Chon id trong data/catalog.js (vd l02). Id chi gom chu thuong, so, dau gach ngang.
2. Sao chep tools/templates/unit-template.js thanh data/l02.js; thay het __ID__ thanh l02; viet noi dung.
   Giu dung ten truong theo mau (id duy nhat, khong doi cau truc).
3. Neu co tro choi: sao chep unit-template-game.js thanh data/l02-game.js (>= 10 vong) va dat "game": true trong catalog.
   Neu co hoat hinh: xem buoc 3b (hoat hinh theo du lieu).
3b. Hoat hinh: sao chep tools/templates/unit-template-anim.js thanh data/l02-anim.js, thay __ID__ thanh l02, sua canh/nhan vat/do vat
   (nhan vat: nam, mai, hanh; xem chu thich trong mau va tools/ANIM-RIG-CONTRACT.md), roi dat "anim": true trong catalog.
   Khong con duong dan "anim": "assets/js/..." (check-units bao loi). Engine/hinh dung chung do trinh nap tu nap khi mo tab Hoat hinh.
   Cau tieng Anh "en" cua moi canh phai bang cac manh "pieces" noi lai; check-units kiem ca dieu nay va BSAnim.validate.
4. Dat anh trong assets/img/l02/ (SVG hoac anh khac). Ten tep phai khop DUNG hoa/thuong voi duong dan trong data.
   Khong dung duong dan ".." hay http://. SVG khong duoc chua <script>, lien ket ra ngoai, <image>.
5. Kiem tra truoc khi mo: node tools/check-units.mjs --unit l02
   (Loi -> exit 1, phai sua. Canh bao -> nen xem lai.)
6. Khi het loi: dat "available": true cho l02 trong data/catalog.js, roi chay: node tools/check-units.mjs
   (khong tham so: kiem catalog va moi unit da mo).
7. Khi unit available:true: them cac file cua unit (data/<id>.js, -game.js, -anim.js, assets/img/<id>/*) vao PRECACHE trong sw.js, roi chay node tools/check-sw.mjs --bump.
8. Muc chua kiem tra: them "v": {"status":"unverified","note":"..."} vao muc do; bo di khi da xac nhan.
UNIT PART 2 (Hoi - dap, id p2-01 ... p2-07)
a. Sao chep tools/templates/unit-template-p2.js thanh data/p2-0N.js (thay __ID__; "unitNo" bang "no" trong catalog).
   Khong dat "game" va "anim" trong catalog (Part 2 khong co hinh); khong can anh, khong can unit-template-game/anim.
b. Moi unit: 24 cau qr (3 bai x 8 cau: theo dang cau hoi, theo bay, tong hop) + 1 bai fill >= 5 cau. Moi dap an SAI co "trap" va "why_vi" (tieng Viet), dap an DUNG khong co hai truong do.
c. Nghe thu tung cau tren thiet bi that (co giong tieng Anh en-US va en-GB) roi moi bo "v": {"status":"unverified"}. check-units --release se LOI neu unit available:true con cau qr unverified.
d. Kiem tra: node tools/check-units.mjs --unit p2-0N   (loi: sai trap/why_vi/so dap an; canh bao: it hon 20 cau, mot chu cai dap an chiem <20% hoac >45%, dap an dung lap tu cua cau hoi).
e. Khi het loi: dat "available": true cho unit trong catalog, them data/p2-0N.js vao PRECACHE trong sw.js, chay node tools/check-sw.mjs --bump, roi node tools/check-units.mjs --release.
Luu y: tien do hoc luu theo id unit trong localStorage - khong doi id sau khi da phat hanh.
