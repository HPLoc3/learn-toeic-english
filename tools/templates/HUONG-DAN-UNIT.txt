HUONG DAN THEM UNIT MOI (vi du: l02)

1. Chon id trong data/catalog.js (vd l02). Id chi gom chu thuong, so, dau gach ngang.
2. Sao chep tools/templates/unit-template.js thanh data/l02.js; thay het __ID__ thanh l02; viet noi dung.
   Giu dung ten truong theo mau (id duy nhat, khong doi cau truc).
3. Neu co tro choi: sao chep unit-template-game.js thanh data/l02-game.js (>= 10 vong) va dat "game": true trong catalog.
   Neu co hoat hinh: xem buoc 3b (hoat hinh theo du lieu).
3b. Hoat hinh: sao chep tools/templates/unit-template-anim.js thanh data/l02-anim.js, thay __ID__ thanh l02, sua canh/nhan vat/do vat
   (nhan vat: nam, mai, hanh; xem chu thich trong mau va tools/ANIM-RIG-CONTRACT.md), roi dat "anim": true trong catalog.
   Khong con duong dan "anim": "assets/js/..." (check-units bao loi). Engine/hinh dung chung do trinh nap tu nap khi mo tab Hoat hinh.
   Cau tieng Anh "en" cua moi canh phai bang cac manh "pieces" noi lai; check-units kiem ca dieu nay va BSAnim.validate.
4. Dat anh trong assets/img/l02/ (SVG hoac anh khac). Ten tep phai khop DUNG hoa/thuong voi duong dan trong data.
   Khong dung duong dan ".." hay http://. SVG khong duoc chua <script>, lien ket ra ngoai, <image>.
5. Kiem tra truoc khi mo: node tools/check-units.mjs --unit l02
   (Loi -> exit 1, phai sua. Canh bao -> nen xem lai.)
6. Khi het loi: dat "available": true cho l02 trong data/catalog.js, roi chay: node tools/check-units.mjs
   (khong tham so: kiem catalog va moi unit da mo).
7. Khi unit available:true: them cac file cua unit (data/<id>.js, -game.js, -anim.js, assets/img/<id>/*) vao PRECACHE trong sw.js, roi chay node tools/check-sw.mjs --bump.
8. Muc chua kiem tra: them "v": {"status":"unverified","note":"..."} vao muc do; bo di khi da xac nhan.
UNIT PART 2 (Hoi - dap, id p2-01 ... p2-07)
a. Sao chep tools/templates/unit-template-p2.js thanh data/p2-0N.js (thay __ID__; "unitNo" bang "no" trong catalog).
   Khong dat "game" va "anim" trong catalog (Part 2 khong co hinh); khong can anh, khong can unit-template-game/anim.
b. Moi unit: 24 cau qr (3 bai x 8 cau: theo dang cau hoi, theo bay, tong hop) + 1 bai fill >= 5 cau. Moi dap an SAI co "trap" va "why_vi" (tieng Viet), dap an DUNG khong co hai truong do.
b2. KHONG de lo dap an: cau hoi va dap an DUNG cua qr khong duoc trung (hoac chua nguyen cau) voi tips (en, q), phrases, sentences (tab Luyen nghe) hay bai fill; viet cau KHAC cung dang. check-units bao LOI. Dap an sai (bay) co the dung lai o noi khac. check-units con so theo TU NOI DUNG (khong can lien nhau): them/bot/dao tu hoac dung tu dong nghia (cut/reduce, buy/purchase...) cung bi canh bao, --release thanh LOI; ngoai le co chu dich khai trong QR_LEAK_ALLOW (check-units.mjs), LUON kem unit vi id cau lap lai o moi unit: "<unit>/<id cau>|*overlap*" (ca cau) hoac "<unit>/<id cau>|*overlap*|<vi tri, vd vocab[3].en>" (mot cho); luat doan lien nhau: "<unit>/<id cau>|<doan tieng Anh chuan hoa>".
c. Nghe thu tung cau tren thiet bi that (co giong tieng Anh en-US va en-GB) roi moi bo "v": {"status":"unverified"}. check-units --release se LOI neu unit available:true con cau qr unverified.
d. Kiem tra: node tools/check-units.mjs --unit p2-0N   (loi: sai trap/why_vi/so dap an; canh bao: it hon 20 cau, mot chu cai dap an chiem <20% hoac >45%, dap an dung lap tu cua cau hoi, cau hoi/dap an dung bi dien dat lai o noi khac ngoai bai qr: gan trung doan lien nhau hoac trung theo tu noi dung, ke ca them/bot/dao tu va tu dong nghia; --release bien canh bao thanh LOI).
e. Khi het loi: dat "available": true cho unit trong catalog, them data/p2-0N.js vao PRECACHE trong sw.js, chay node tools/check-sw.mjs --bump, roi node tools/check-units.mjs --release.
UNIT PART 3 / PART 4 (Hoi thoai / bai noi, id p3-01 ... p3-07, p4-01 ... p4-07)
a. Sao chep tools/templates/unit-template-p3p4.js thanh data/p3-0N.js hoac data/p4-0N.js (thay __ID__; "unitNo" bang "no" trong catalog; "part" dung ten Part).
   Khong dat "game" va "anim" trong catalog (khong co hinh minh hoa); khong can anh, khong can audio.
b. Moi unit: 4 tips, 24 vocab, 10 phrases, 8 sentences, 6 doan (set) x 3 cau = 18 cau conv chia thanh 2 bai (c1: doan 1-3, c2: doan 4-6), 2 doan co "graphic", 1 bai fill >= 5 cau.
   Moi doan: kind "conversation" (2-3 nguoi, khac gioi tinh hoac accent) hoac "talk" (1 nguoi + talk_type); moi cau co 4 dap an A-D, "evidence" (chi so dong chua dap an),
   dap an SAI co "trap" + "why_vi", dap an DUNG khong co hai truong do; cau "intent" co "quote" nguyen van; cau dung hinh co "uses_graphic": true va q.en bat dau "Look at the graphic".
   Noi dung phai la GOC (ten nguoi, cong ty, dia diem, so lieu tu dat), khong chep de thi/sach.
c. KHONG de lo: cau trong loi thoai (>= 5 tu) va dap an DUNG (>= 4 tu) khong duoc xuat hien (nguyen van hoac gan giong) o tips, phrases, sentences, intro_vi, vocab, bai fill:
   dung tinh huong KHAC o cac muc do. check-units bao LOI (ke ca --unit); --release bien canh bao gan trung thanh LOI. Ngoai le: QR_LEAK_ALLOW, vd "p3-03/c1-s2|*overlap*|phrases[4].en".
d. Kiem tra: node tools/check-units.mjs --unit p3-0N   (loi: cau truc, trap/why_vi, evidence, quote, hinh, lo loi thoai; canh bao: doan ngoai 60-160 tu, cau > 160 ky tu, < 6 doan, chua co doan co hinh,
   chu cai dap an ngoai 15-35%, 3 cau cung dap an, dap an dung chep nguyen van tu loi thoai, cau hinh co dap an dung nam trong loi thoai hoac khong la o cua hinh; --release: cac canh bao ve noi dung thanh LOI).
   Nghe thu tung doan tren thiet bi that (giong en-US, en-GB, en-AU), mo unit.html?u=p3-0N&edit=1 tren localhost, roi bo "v": {"status":"unverified"} (--release LOI neu con trong doan, cau hoac dap an).
e. Khi het loi: dat "available": true trong catalog, them data/p3-0N.js vao PRECACHE trong sw.js, doi VERSION va chay node tools/check-sw.mjs --write, khi mo them unit thi thu hep cac mau ^data\/p[34]-0[3-7] (va ^data\/p2-0[3-7]) trong tools/test-sw.mjs,
   roi node tools/check-units.mjs --release. Khong doi id sau khi da phat hanh.
