# Image credits

The scene images used by the animated lessons are composed from two parts:

- People and the items they hold: the characters (a barista, an office worker,
  a man in a navy suit and a flower-shop owner) are original characters created
  by the site owner with an AI image-generation tool, then cut out from their
  original backgrounds. The items they hold or sit at (takeaway cup, mug,
  notebook and pen, phone, handbags, bouquet, and the table and chairs of the
  meeting scene) come from those same AI images.
- Backgrounds and props (cafe, office, lobby, street and flower-shop rooms, the
  counters including the nam-serve and trio-line counters, desk, laptop, flower
  shelf): drawn procedurally in flat style by `tools/compose-scenes.py`
  (Pillow only), so no third-party artwork is used.

No third-party characters, brands, logos or other third-party intellectual
property are used or imitated.

Files are produced with `tools/compose-scenes.py` (cut-outs + drawn backgrounds,
1600x900) and `tools/make-scenes.py` (resized to 800x450 and 1600x900, WebP,
metadata stripped). This file is not precached by the service worker.

The Unit 3 scene images are whole AI-generated pictures made by the site owner, not cut-out composites of separate parts.

Status: Unit 1 and Unit 2 scene images are final art (date 2026-11-19). The Unit 2 SVG exercise pictures are original flat vector drawings. Unit 3 scene images obj-table, obj-boxes, obj-bike, obj-lamp and obj-books (assets/img/l03/scene, assets/img-hd/l03) are final art generated by the site owner (original objects and settings, date 2026-11-19). obj-cars and the outro obj-outro (a 3x2 grid of the six scenes, to be built with tools/make-outro-grid.py) are still temporary PLACEHOLDERS (gradient plus scene name, no artwork) until the owner supplies a new cars image; check-units --release fails while data/l03-anim.js has placeholder: true. The Unit 3 SVG exercise pictures are original flat vector drawings. Unit 4 scene images (assets/img/l04/scene, assets/img-hd/l04) are temporary PLACEHOLDERS (gradient plus scene name, no artwork) and must be replaced with final art supplied by the owner before release (check-units --release fails while data/l04-anim.js has placeholder: true). The Unit 4 SVG exercise pictures are original flat vector drawings.
